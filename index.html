  1. Concept site · Fragrance · 2026

    Quatréa

    Four perfumes, one for each season, told through scroll

    Brief
    Show what a fragrance site can feel like when the product never leaves the screen: four seasonal perfumes on one continuous 3D stage.
    Built
    One Three.js canvas stays mounted across every page, so the four glass bottles (real-time refraction, a liquid fill level, a cap for each season) glide between layouts. GSAP ScrollTrigger runs pinned season chapters, a horizontal collection, a notes pyramid where the bottle evaporates, and a scent-finder quiz the 3D scene reacts to.
    Result
    Live in two editions from one codebase. The standard edition detects the GPU and lowers quality when frames drop, and an Ultra edition runs at full fidelity on high-end machines.
    • Three.js
    • React Three Fiber
    • GSAP ScrollTrigger
    • GLSL
    • Lenis

  2. Shopify app · DTF printing · 2026

    Gang Sheet Builder

    Print-ready DTF sheets from the product page

    Brief
    Let print shops sell DTF gang sheets without emailing files back and forth: customers upload artwork, size it and order, and production gets a file it can print.
    Built
    An embedded React Router app with a Konva editor in a theme app block. Designs auto-nest onto the shortest sheet that fits, with background removal, crop, curved text, low-resolution warnings and Canva import. When an order is paid, a background worker renders a print-ready PDF and a 300 DPI PNG, and admin extensions put the files on the order page.
    Result
    Deployed with Mad Monkey Transfers as the first client, plus a vinyl-banner builder priced from their spec. The App Store listing and reviewer steps are ready for submission.
    • React Router

  10. Custom cart · Halal supplements · 2026

    Deen Health

    A custom cart that sells the routine

    Brief
    Replace the theme's basic cart with one that moves customers from a one-time purchase to a subscription, and from a 30-day to a 90- or 180-day supply, without leaving the cart.
    Built
    A custom cart drawer (Liquid section, ~590 lines of JavaScript, CSS) that takes over every add-to-cart form and header cart link. Customers switch one-time or subscription plans, Single, Duo or Family sets and quantities in place. It shows one offer at a time (one-time → monthly, then 30 → 90 → 180 days) with the saving worked out from catalogue prices, so discount codes don't distort it, and remembers when someone declines. Product recommendations carry their own purchase options, with a free-shipping message by country.
    Result
    Live on deenhealth.co. Every change runs through one queue that locks the cart and recovers if it changed elsewhere. It's keyboard and screen-reader friendly (focus trap, Escape, status announcements), and every offer is editable from the theme editor.
    • Liquid

    • Cart AJAX API
    • Accessibility
